## The opportunity

Most organizations still mix WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, and email threads to approve leave. That breaks down when you have:

- Multiple departments and job roles  
- Managers, HR, and executives who each need a turn  
- Supporting documents (medical notes, etc.)  
- Hundreds of employees to onboard  
- A brand that should feel like *your* company — not a generic SaaS skin  

This product is a full **leave operating system**: request → review → final decision → notify → report — with structure you can adapt to your business.

---

## Why organizations choose it

### Built to match *your* hierarchy — not a fixed template

Approval is multi-stage and configurable:

1. **Employee** submits a request  
2. **Manager** (optional) can review first if you turn that on  
3. **HR** reviews policy and documents  
4. **Executive roles** (e.g. Director / General Manager equivalents) give the **final** approve or deny  

Until the final stage completes, the request stays **pending**. Leadership stays in control.

### Departments and roles you define

You are not locked into one org chart.

- Create, edit, activate/deactivate, or remove **departments**  
- Use standard permission levels (Employee, Manager, HR, Executive, Super Admin)  
- Create **custom role names** for your business (e.g. “Branch Lead”, “Clinic Admin”, “Site Supervisor”) and map them to the right access level  
- Assign every person to the right department and reporting manager  

When you sell or deploy this for another company, departments and roles are reshaped to that business — same engine, their structure.

### White-label ready

A Super Admin **System Studio** lets you change:

- Logo and colors  
- Company name and product name  
- Login and landing page text  
- Which modules appear in the menu  
- Feature switches (self-service leave, manager-first approval, reports, bulk import, and more)  

Prospects see *their* brand from day one.

### Documents where decisions happen

If a leave type requires evidence, employees upload it with the request. Authorized reviewers can **view** or **download** the file inside the system — no separate file share.

### Notifications people actually see

- In-app alerts when a request needs action  
- Alerts when a request is approved or denied  
- Welcome message when an account is created  
- Reminder when leave is about to start  

Plus **official email** for account creation (with temporary password and login link) and for final leave decisions — once SMTP is connected to the organization’s mail.

### Scale and reporting

- Bulk Excel/CSV import with one-time credentials for new staff  
- Excel exports (users, all requests, pending, denied)  
- Dashboard trends, calendar, team coverage, and charts  
- Searchable approval queues (waiting / done / denied)  

### Country-aware policies

Leave entitlements, holidays, and working-day calculations can follow the employee’s country — useful for regional or multi-country employers.

### Security by default

Password rules, temporary password forced change, CSRF protection, session control, login lockout, role-based access, and audit logging.

## Sample day-one journey (any company)

1. Super Admin creates the organization account and branding  
2. HR defines departments and roles  
3. Staff are added or bulk-imported  
4. Employees request leave (with documents when needed)  
5. Approvers act in the queue; employees get notified and emailed  
6. Leadership exports Excel or opens reports when needed
